MindReps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MindReps ("the app") is a mental math training game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. MindReps is meant for everyone, including children.

In short: your progress is backed up so it isn't lost, signing in is optional, and you can delete your account and its data from the app at any time.

Your progress and backup

Your scores, streaks, practice history and settings are stored on your device. Once you have answered the birth year question, the app also backs your progress up to Google Firebase, so it can be restored on a new phone. Before you sign in, the backup is tied only to a random ID that Firebase creates for your device; it has no name or contact details.

The backup contains your streaks, scores and personal bests, your practice history (including questions you got wrong, so you can practise them), your chosen theme and your birth year. Sound, language and reminder settings stay on your device.

Accounts

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple, Google or an email address and password, so your progress and MindReps Plus follow you to another device. When you sign in we keep your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and, if your Apple or Google account shares it, your name. We use them only to sign you in and, for parents, to send the confirmation described below. We never send marketing email.

Firebase stores this information on Google Cloud servers in the United States. It is kept until you delete your account. See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Deleting your data

Open Account in the app and tap Delete account. This deletes your backed-up progress and your account, and clears your progress from the device. If you never signed in, sign in first and then delete the account, and the backup from your device is deleted with it. You can also ask us to delete your data using the contact address below.

Your birth year

The app asks for your birth year once, to decide which kind of ads to show and whether account sign-in is for a parent. It is part of your backup, as above. The app tells Google whether a player should get child-safe, teen or general ads; your birth year itself is not shared with advertisers.

Ads

The free version of MindReps shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type, operating system, app activity related to ads, and an advertising identifier. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Players under 13: ads are marked as child-directed. Google does not show personalized ads, does not use the advertising identifier for tracking, and limits ads to content suitable for children. The app does not show tracking or consent prompts to these players.
  • Players 13 and over: on iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when Apple asks. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, you can accept or decline personalized ads in a consent form and change your choice later.

Subscriptions

MindReps Plus removes ads. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. To check whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipts and an app user ID: a random one, or your account's ID once you sign in so Plus works on your other devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us.

Children's privacy

Players who enter a birth year that makes them 13 or under can't sign in themselves. Their progress is backed up only under the random device ID described above, which we use only to keep the app working (restoring progress), never for ads or tracking.

A parent or guardian can sign in for their child. Before signing in, the app asks for a parent check and shows the parent what is kept, and the parent agrees. We then email the parent a confirmation with a link to withdraw consent, which deletes the child's backed-up progress and the account. A parent can also delete it at any time from Account in the app, or by contacting us.

We never ask a child for their name, email or other contact details. The app has no chat, sharing or social features, and we don't share a child's progress with anyone.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Remove all ads by subscribing to MindReps Plus.
  • Sign in or not: the app works the same either way.
  • Delete your account from Account in the app to remove your backed-up progress.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
